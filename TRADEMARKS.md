# BRBuddy name and icon

BRBuddy's **code** is open source under the [MIT licence](LICENSE): use it, change it, fork it, ship your own app
built on it. That's the point.

The **name "BRBuddy"** and the **BRBuddy app icon** (Ember with the "BRB" bubble, in `Config/AppIcon.icns`,
`Config/AppIcon-1024.png` and the website's icon images) are not covered by the MIT licence. They belong to Andy Rana
and identify the official app, so people can tell it apart from the many versions others are free to make.

## Fine without asking
- Forking the repository, building it and running it yourself.
- Saying your app is "based on BRBuddy" or "a fork of BRBuddy", with a link back.
- Writing about BRBuddy, reviewing it, or linking to it, using its name and icon to mean the official app.
- Packaging the official, unmodified releases (for example in a package manager), under the BRBuddy name.

## Please don't
- Publish or distribute a modified version under the name BRBuddy, or a confusingly similar name.
- Use the BRBuddy icon, or something close to it, for your own app.
- Suggest your version is official, endorsed by, or made by the BRBuddy project.

If you ship your own version, give it its own name and icon, keep the MIT copyright notice in [LICENSE](LICENSE),
and you're good. The companions drawn in code (Ember, Pebble, Prism, Comet, Drift, Gremlin) are part of the code and
come with it under MIT; just don't use Ember with the BRB bubble as your app's icon.

Not sure? Ask at hello@dhaataa.com. We'll usually say yes.
