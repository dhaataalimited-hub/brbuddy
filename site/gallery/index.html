Add it to BRBuddy

  1. Download it and double-click the .zip to unzip it.
  2. In BRBuddy, open Settings → Companion → Add companion pack… and pick the folder.
  3. Choose it in the Companion picker. Click it to boop it.

Make your own

Two ways to build a companion.

No drawing skills needed for either.

The easy way

Companion Studio

Built into BRBuddy. Pick a body, colours, ears, extras, a face, blush and a mouth, then its own boop sound and catchphrase. It reacts in a live preview while you build.

  1. Open Settings → Companion → Companion Studio…
  2. Build your critter and boop it to test it.
  3. Click Save as pack. It's yours.

Anything you can imagine

Ask any AI

A toaster, a cactus, a sleepy moon. Copy our prompt, fill in the three lines under MY CHARACTER, and paste it into an AI that can run code and make files (Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini). It hands you a pack folder to add.

Share yours

Put your companion in the gallery.

Made something you love? Send it in and it could keep someone else company while their agent works.

Step 1

Zip it up

In BRBuddy's Companion settings, click Show companions folder. Right-click your companion's folder and choose Compress.

Step 2

Send it in

Fill in the short form on GitHub: its name, how to credit you, and the zip. No GitHub account? Email it to us instead, with the zip attached.

Step 3

We put it up

A person checks every companion before it goes live, usually within a week. We'll reply on your submission, or to your email, when it's up.
